# Ordering food

## How to place an order
Browse the menu on the home page and press the + button on a dish to add it to your cart. Open the cart (the basket icon at the top), check the items and press Proceed to Checkout. Fill in your delivery details (name, email, street, city, state, zip code, country and phone number), choose how to pay (Cash on delivery or card with Stripe) and press Proceed To Payment. You need to be signed in to place an order. If you are not, press the sign in button at the top.

## Changing the cart
Press + on a dish to add one more, and the minus button to remove one. In the cart page the x button removes a dish completely. The cart is saved to your account, so it is still there when you come back later.

## Tracking an order
Open My Orders from the profile icon at the top. Every order shows its items, the total, and its status. Press Track Order to refresh the status. You also get an email when an order is confirmed, when a payment fails, and when the status changes (for example Out for delivery or Delivered).

## Order statuses
Payment Pending: you chose card payment and the payment is not finished yet. Payment Failed: the payment did not go through, so the order was not placed. Food Processing: the order is confirmed and the kitchen is preparing it. Out for delivery: the order is on its way. Delivered: the order has arrived.

## Changing or cancelling an order
The app has no cancel or edit button for an order that is already placed. Please contact us using the contact details at the bottom of the website as soon as possible.
