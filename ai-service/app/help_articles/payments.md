# Payments and prices

## Payment methods
There are two ways to pay. Cash on delivery (COD): you pay when the food arrives and the order is confirmed straight away. Card with Stripe: you are taken to Stripe's secure payment page and pay with a credit or debit card. We never see or store your card details, Stripe handles them.

## Delivery fee and total
All prices are in Indian rupees (the rupee sign). Every order has a flat delivery fee of 50 rupees. The cart page shows the subtotal (the dishes), the delivery fee and the total you pay.

## If a card payment fails or you leave the payment page
The order is only placed once the payment succeeds. You have 35 minutes to finish paying on the Stripe page. If you close the page or the time runs out, the payment is cancelled, the order is marked Payment Failed (or never confirmed), the dishes you held are released, and your cart is still saved so you can try again whenever you like. You are not charged for an order that was not confirmed. If money was taken from your account and the order still shows Payment Failed, contact us with your order number.

## Order number
Each order has an order number. The first 8 characters of the order id, shown in your emails as a number with a hash sign, are enough to find your order when you contact us.
