# Availability and stock

## Out of stock dishes
A dish marked Out of stock is greyed out and cannot be added to the cart. It means every available portion has been taken right now. It may come back later when the kitchen adds more stock, so check again later.

## Not enough portions left
Some dishes have a limited number of portions. If you ask for more than are left, checkout stops and tells you, for example, that only 1 is left. Nothing is charged and your cart is kept. Lower the quantity or remove the dish and try again.

## A dish in your cart became out of stock
If a dish in your cart sells out while you are shopping, the cart page marks it as out of stock. Remove it from the cart and continue with the rest.

## Dishes that are no longer on the menu
If the restaurant removes a dish that is in your cart, checkout tells you that some items are no longer available and removes them from the cart. Check your cart and try again.

## Reserved portions
When you place an order the portions are held for you. They are only given up for good once the order is confirmed. If a card payment is not completed, the portions go back so another customer can order them.
