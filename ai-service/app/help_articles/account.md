# Account and help

## Signing in
Press the sign in button at the top of the website to create an account or log in with your email and password. You need an account to place orders and to see My Orders. Passwords are never shown to anyone, including us.

## Delivery time
The app does not show an estimated delivery time. Follow your order in My Orders and in the emails we send. The status changes to Out for delivery when it is on its way.

## Contact and support
The contact details (phone number and email) are at the bottom of every page on the website. When you contact us, please give your order number.

## What this assistant can and cannot do
The assistant can answer questions about the menu, prices, availability, payments, delivery fees and how ordering works. It cannot place orders, change or cancel orders, take payments, or look up anyone's orders or personal details. For those, use the website or contact us.
